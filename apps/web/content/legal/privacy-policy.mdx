# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: April 1, 2026**

Koya ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our services.

## Information We Collect

We may collect information about you in a variety of ways, including:

### Personal Data
- Name, email address, and phone number
- Government-issued identification for KYC compliance
- Financial information necessary for processing conversions

### Usage Data
- Device information and browser type
- IP address and location data
- Pages visited and features used

## How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:
- Process your currency conversions
- Comply with legal and regulatory requirements (KYC/AML)
- Improve our services and user experience
- Communicate with you about your account and transactions
- Detect and prevent fraud

## Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

## Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at hello@koyabank.com.
